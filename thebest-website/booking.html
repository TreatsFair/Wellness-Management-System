Booking Terms & Conditions

Booking & Confirmation

Please check your outlet, service, date and time carefully before paying. Your selected slot is held for 10 minutes while you complete payment — your appointment is only confirmed once payment is successful and a booking confirmation is shown. Please keep your booking reference for any enquiries.

Prices & Payment

All prices are in Malaysian Ringgit and any applicable SST is shown before you pay. Payments are processed securely through Billplz — we do not collect or store your card, bank or e-wallet login details. A pending, failed or abandoned payment does not confirm a booking.

Cancellations, No-Shows & Refunds

Payments are final and non-refundable for cancellations, no-shows, late arrivals we can't accommodate, incorrect booking details, or a change of mind. To reschedule, please contact your selected outlet as early as possible — this is subject to availability. If we need to cancel or change your appointment, we'll offer a new time or an equivalent service in exchange.

Late Arrival

Please arrive at least 10 minutes early. We will only hold your slot up to 10 minutes after your scheduled time. If you arrive late, your service may be shortened or rescheduled to avoid delaying other customers. The appointment end time will only be extended when staff and room availability allow.

Health & Conduct

Please tell our staff about any medical condition, injury, pregnancy or allergy that could affect your treatment — our services are not a substitute for medical care. We may refuse or stop a service for safety reasons, or where a customer behaves inappropriately toward staff or other customers; payment is non-refundable in these cases.

Personal Information

We may collect your name, contact information, booking detailsand booking status you provide to manage your appointment, process payment (via Billplz) and contact you about your booking. We keep this information secure and only use it for these purposes.

Booking Enquiries

For any booking enquires, please contact your selected outlet directly.
